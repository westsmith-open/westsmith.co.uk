# Word Import for monday.com

Turn your Word files into monday docs, with their headings, lists, tables,
links and formatting.

[See the listing on the monday.com marketplace](https://monday.com/marketplace/listing/10000675/word-import)

## Ways to import

* **Into a new doc.** Open **Word Import** under **Workspace apps** in your
  workspace and choose a Word file. You get a new doc, named after the file
  or whatever you like.
* **Many files at once.** Choose several Word files, or a whole folder, and
  each one becomes a doc, together in a new folder.
* **Into the doc you're in.** In any doc, click **+** and choose Word Import.
  Put the file where you clicked, at the end or in place of what's there.
* **From your boards.** Word files in a board's Files column become a doc on
  each item, in the board's Doc column. Pick items, a whole group or a
  single item from its menu.

monday sends you a notification when your import is done, and the **Word
import jobs** page lists every import, so you can find the doc, import a
newer version of the file or tidy up.

## What's imported

* Headings, titles, quotes and code
* Bold, italic, underline, strikethrough and text colours
* Bulleted, numbered and checklists, with their indentation
* Tables, including merged cells and header rows
* Links
* Footnotes and endnotes, text in text boxes, equations and tracked
  changes as they'll read once accepted

Pictures aren't imported yet.
