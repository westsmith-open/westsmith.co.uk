# AdsAdmin

A cleaner, read-only sidebar for Google Ads. Built for small business owners and freelancers running their own campaigns, not agencies.

*Coming soon to the Chrome Web Store.*

## What it does

Google Ads is cluttered with recommendations and prompts designed to increase spend, not help you. AdsAdmin reads the page you're on, hides the noise, and tells you what actually matters. It never touches your account.

## Features

* Page-by-page guidance: plain-English advice for whatever Google Ads screen you're looking at
* Hide recommendations, budget alerts, optimization score, and Smart campaign prompts
* A built-in PPC guide covering keyword strategy, bidding, Quality Score, and what to ignore
* Read-only campaign navigation: no editing, no bulk actions, no API access

## Privacy

AdsAdmin doesn't collect, store, or transmit any of your data. It runs entirely inside your browser, reading the Google Ads page you're already on. See the [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy) for details.
