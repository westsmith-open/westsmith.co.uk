# AdsAdmin Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22nd July 2026

1. Introduction

    We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information.

2. Information We Collect

    We don't collect any information. AdsAdmin is a read-only Chrome extension that runs entirely inside your browser. It reads the Google Ads page you already have open to display guidance and hide clutter. It never sends any data to us or to any third party.

3. Data Storage and Security

    AdsAdmin stores your extension preferences (which features are toggled on or off) locally in your browser using Chrome's built-in storage. This never leaves your device and contains no personal or account data.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

    We do not sell your personal information to third parties. None is ever collected, so there is nothing to sell.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

    We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

6. Contact Us

    If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: info@westsmith.co.uk
