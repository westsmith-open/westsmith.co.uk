# Chimney Chase

<img src="/static/images/apps/chimney-chase-icon.png" alt="Chimney Chase app icon" width="160" height="160" style="border-radius: 28px;">

A Christmas delivery run, one tap at a time. Wait for the aiming beam to turn green, then launch Santa's sleigh towards the next chimney. Deliver a present and watch the house light up.

Explore snowy rooftops, cobbled streets and decorated Christmas trees. Every ten deliveries brings a new level. Play on iPhone or iPad, including landscape on iPad.

The whole game is free and works offline. No purchases, no account, no tracking and no third-party adverts. Includes links to our other games.
