# Chimney Chase Support

For help, email [info@westsmith.co.uk](mailto:info@westsmith.co.uk).

## Playing

Tap Start delivering. Santa circles the current chimney. Wait for the dotted beam to turn green, then tap anywhere on the rooftops to launch. On iPad landscape, you can also use Launch sleigh.

A successful arrival delivers one present. Every ten deliveries advances the level. Missing a target or scrolling off the bottom ends the run.

## Sound

Tap the speaker button to switch music and effects on or off. Check your device volume and silent mode if you cannot hear the game.

## Pause

Tap Pause to stop the run, then Resume to continue. The game also pauses when you leave the app.

## Scores and price

Your best score stays on your device. Deleting the app removes its score and settings. There are no accounts or online leaderboards.

The whole game is free. There are no purchases or third-party adverts. Menus include links to other Westsmith games.
