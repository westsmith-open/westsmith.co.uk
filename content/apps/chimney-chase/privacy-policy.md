# Chimney Chase Privacy Policy

Chimney Chase is made by Westsmith Ltd. This policy covers the iPhone, iPad and Android apps.

## Your game data

You do not need an account to play. We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. Your best score, rounds played, sound setting and haptic preference are stored on your device. We do not receive them.

Deleting the app removes its score and settings.

## Purchases

Chimney Chase is free and has no purchases.

## Ratings

After some rounds, the game asks Apple or Google Play to show an optional rating prompt. The store decides whether the prompt appears. If you choose to leave a rating or review, the store receives it and handles it under its own privacy policy. We do not receive ratings or review text through the app. Public reviews are visible on the relevant store.

## Links to other games

The app includes links to other Westsmith games. Tapping a link opens the App Store or Google Play. The game does not record which links you tap. These services operate under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can delete a Google Play review through your Google Play account.

## Support

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to answer your request and keep the correspondence only as long as needed to provide support and meet applicable record-keeping obligations. Do not include passwords or payment details.

For privacy questions or requests to delete support correspondence, contact [info@westsmith.co.uk](mailto:info@westsmith.co.uk).
