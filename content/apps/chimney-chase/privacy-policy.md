# Chimney Chase Privacy Policy

Chimney Chase is made by Westsmith Ltd. This policy covers the iPhone and iPad app.

## Your game data

You do not need an account to play. We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. Your best score, rounds played and sound setting are stored on your device. We do not receive them.

Deleting the app removes its score and settings.

## Purchases

Chimney Chase is free and has no purchases.

## Ratings

After some rounds, the game asks Apple to show its optional rating prompt. Apple decides whether the prompt appears and handles any rating under its own privacy policy.

## Links to other games

The app includes links to other Westsmith games. Tapping a link opens the App Store. The game does not record which links you tap. Apple's services operate under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Support

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to answer your request and keep the correspondence only as long as needed to provide support and meet applicable record-keeping obligations. Do not include passwords or payment details.

For privacy questions or requests to delete support correspondence, contact [info@westsmith.co.uk](mailto:info@westsmith.co.uk).
