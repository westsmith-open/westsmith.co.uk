# Defuse Support

For help with Defuse, email [info@westsmith.co.uk](mailto:info@westsmith.co.uk) and we'll get back to you.

## Common questions

**What does "No Guess" mean?**
Every No Guess board is checked by a solver before you see it. There is always a logical next move, so you never have to guess between two tiles. Classic mode uses random boards like the original game.

**How do I flag a mine?**
Long-press a tile, or switch the toggle at the bottom of the screen to Flag and tap.

**How do I restore Defuse Pro on a new device?**
Open Settings (the palette icon on the home screen) and tap Restore purchases.
