# Defuse Privacy Policy

Defuse is made by Westsmith Ltd. This policy covers the iPhone, iPad and Android versions.

## Your game data

Defuse does not collect, store or transmit any personal data.

- You don't need an account
- There's no analytics, tracking or advertising
- Works without an internet connection after download
- Best times and settings stay on your device

## Purchases

Apple or Google Play handles purchases of Defuse Pro, depending on your device. The game checks purchase information with that store to unlock Pro and restore purchases. We never see your card or bank details. Purchase records follow the store's own privacy policy.

- [Apple privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)
- [Google privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## Support

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include. We use it to answer your request and keep it only as long as we need to. Please don't include passwords or payment details.

For privacy questions, contact [info@westsmith.co.uk](mailto:info@westsmith.co.uk).
