# Defuse Privacy Policy

Defuse does not collect, store or transmit any personal data.

- You don't need an account
- There's no analytics, tracking or advertising
- Works without an internet connection after download
- Best times and settings stay on your device
- Apple handles purchases of Defuse Pro. We never see your payment details.

For questions, contact [info@westsmith.co.uk](mailto:info@westsmith.co.uk).
