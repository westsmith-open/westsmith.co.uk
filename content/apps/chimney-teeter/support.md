# Chimney Teeter Support

For help, email [info@westsmith.co.uk](mailto:info@westsmith.co.uk).

## Playing

Tap the playfield or Tilt roofs to reverse every snowy slate. Guide presents into clear chimneys to earn the points shown. Smoking chimneys deduct one or three points. Father Christmas arrives every fifth delivery and earns five times the positive points. A round ends after 30 deliveries or five smoky landings.

## Sound and touch

Open Settings to switch Christmas music, sound effects and haptics on or off. Check your device volume if you cannot hear the game. On iPhone and iPad, check silent mode too.

## Scores

The best score stays on your device. Deleting the app removes its scores and settings. There are no accounts or online leaderboards.

## Price

The whole game is free. There are no adverts or purchases.
