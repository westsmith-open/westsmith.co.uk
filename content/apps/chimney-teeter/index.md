# Chimney Teeter

A rooftop Christmas for iPhone, iPad and Android. Tap to tilt the snowy slates and guide presents into clear chimneys. Avoid the smoke and look out for Father Christmas.

Play short rounds, beat your best score and enjoy the Christmas music. No adverts, no purchases and no account. Works offline.
