# Nine Support

For help with Nine, email [info@westsmith.co.uk](mailto:info@westsmith.co.uk) and we'll get back to you.

## Common questions

**Do puzzles ever need guessing?**
No. Every puzzle has exactly one solution and is checked by a solver before you see it, so it can always be finished by logic alone.

**How do I pencil in notes?**
Tap Notes below the grid, then tap numbers to add or remove them. Notes clear themselves when you place a number in the same row, column or box.

**I left a puzzle half-finished. Where is it?**
Nine saves automatically. Tap the difficulty on the home screen marked Resume.

**How do I restore Nine Pro on a new device?**
Open Settings (the palette icon on the home screen) and tap Restore purchases.
