# Patience Support

For help with Patience, email [info@westsmith.co.uk](mailto:info@westsmith.co.uk) and we'll get back to you.

## Common questions

**What does Winnable mean?**
In Winnable mode, every deal is checked by a solver before you see it, so it can always be won. You can still play yourself into a corner, though, so a winnable deal can be lost. Classic mode deals random hands, like the original, and some of those can't be won.

**The hint says "No way forward". What now?**
It means no sequence of moves can win the game from where you are. Undo a few moves to find another way, or start a new deal. Undo history is kept even if you leave the game.

**How do I change the table or turn off the music?**
Pick a table on the home screen, or tap Table during a game. Music, sounds and haptics can be switched on or off on the home screen, and the speaker button in a game turns the music on or off.

**The cards are hard to read. Can I make them bigger?**
Turn on Large cards on the home screen, or tap the card style button during a game. Large cards show a bold rank and suit, which is easier to read in Spider.

**I left a game half-finished. Where is it?**
Patience saves automatically. Choose the same game on the home screen and tap Resume.

**How do I restore Patience Pro on a new device?**
Tap Restore purchases at the bottom of the home screen.
