# Bob Stackit Privacy Policy

Bob Stackit is made by Westsmith Ltd. This policy covers the iPhone, iPad and Android versions.

## Your game data

You do not need an account to play. We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs in the game. Your best score, most lines, games played and settings are stored on your device. We do not receive any of it.

Deleting the app removes its scores and settings.

## Purchases

Bob Stackit is free and has no purchases.

## Ratings

Now and then the game asks Apple or Google Play to show a rating prompt. Apple or Google decides whether it appears and handles any rating you leave, under their own privacy policies.

- [Apple privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)
- [Google privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## Links to other games

The home screen links to other Westsmith games in the App Store or Google Play. Tapping one opens the store. The game does not record which links you tap.

## Support

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to answer your request and keep the correspondence only as long as needed to provide support and meet applicable record-keeping obligations. Do not include passwords or payment details.

For privacy questions or requests to delete support correspondence, contact [info@westsmith.co.uk](mailto:info@westsmith.co.uk).
