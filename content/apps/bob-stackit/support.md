# Bob Stackit Support

For help with Bob Stackit, email [info@westsmith.co.uk](mailto:info@westsmith.co.uk) and we'll get back to you.

## Common questions

**How do I play?**
Christmas pieces fall onto the card one at a time. Use left and right to slide a piece, rotate to turn it and soft drop or hard drop to bring it down. Fill a row from edge to edge and it clears. The game ends when there is no room for the next piece.

**How does scoring work?**
Clearing one, two, three or four rows at once scores 100, 300, 500 or 800 points, times your level. Soft drop adds a point for each row and hard drop adds two.

**It's too fast. Can I slow it down?**
Turn on Gentle mode in Settings. The pieces then fall at the starting speed all the way through.

**How do I change the picture or turn off the music?**
Open Settings from the gear button on the home screen. Pick one of three Christmas card backdrops and switch music, sound effects and haptics on or off.

**Is it really free?**
Yes. There are no adverts and nothing to buy.
