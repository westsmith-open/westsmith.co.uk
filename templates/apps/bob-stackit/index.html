A Victorian Christmas falling blocks game. Robins, plum puddings, holly, baubles, bells and red ribbons fall onto an old Christmas card. Complete a row and the bells ring as it clears.

Completely free. No adverts, no purchases, no account and no tracking.

Features

  • Seven Christmas motifs and three Victorian card backdrops
  • Big buttons for two thumbs, with hold to repeat
  • A ghost piece and a preview of the next piece
  • Gentle mode for a slower, relaxed game
  • Strings, sleigh bells and chimes, each with its own switch
  • No account, no data collected, no internet required
