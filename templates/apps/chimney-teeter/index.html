Free on the App Store for iPhone and iPad. Coming soon to Android.

A rooftop Christmas, one tap at a time. Tilt the snowy slates and guide presents into clear chimneys. Avoid the smoke and look out for Father Christmas.

Play short rounds, beat your best score and enjoy the Christmas music. Father Christmas arrives every fifth delivery and earns five times the positive points.

The whole game is free. No adverts, no purchases, no account and no tracking. Works offline.

Features

  • One tap tilts every snowy roof
  • Thirty deliveries or five smoky landings to finish a round
  • Illustrated winter rooftops and Christmas music
  • Separate switches for music, sound effects and haptics
  • Pause whenever you need a break
